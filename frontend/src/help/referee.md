# Referee guide

{{appName}} tells you which games you're working, where they are, and who you're working with. On game day you check in from your phone at the gym, which is what gets you paid.

{{gettingStarted}}

## My games

**Menu → My games.** Your upcoming games, each with the date and time, the division and teams, the gym and court, a **Directions** link, and who you're working with. The top line shows how many games you have and what you've earned this season.

![My games on a phone](/help/img/ref-my-games.png)

You'll get an email whenever you're put on a game, taken off one, or a game you're working moves.

## Checking in on game day

Check-in opens **60 minutes before tip-off** and closes **3 hours after**. When it's open, a **Check-in is open** card appears at the top of My games.

1. At the gym, choose **I'm here: check in**.
2. Your phone may ask to share your location. That confirms you're at the gym, but check-in still works if you say no.

![Checking in](/help/img/ref-check-in.png)

**Checked in** appears on the game, and it counts toward your pay. If you couldn't check in, let the Referee Assignor know, and they can confirm you worked it.

## Can't make a game?

Before game day, choose **Can't make it** on the game, give a short reason, and **Decline game**. The assignor is told straight away and finds someone else. On game day itself, contact the assignor directly.

![Declining a game](/help/img/ref-cant-make-it.png)

## Dates you can't work

Under **Dates I can't work**, choose **Add dates**, pick the first and last day, and add an optional note (e.g. *Out of town*). You won't be assigned on those dates. If you're already on a game in that range, you'll be reminded to decline it, and the assignor is told. Choose **Remove** if your plans change.

![Adding dates you can't work](/help/img/ref-dates.png)

## Past games and pay

**Past games** lists the games you've worked, each marked **Worked**, **No-show**, or **Not confirmed**, with the pay for each worked game.

**Who pays you.** The programs pay the referees, one each: the home team's program pays Referee 1 and the away team's program pays Referee 2. Each upcoming game shows **Who pays you**: the program to expect payment from. If you were the only referee on a game, it shows both programs, *half each*. A guest team's club pays its referee the same way, and is marked *(guest)*. Payments are made outside the app.

## Getting paid

Programs pay you directly; no money moves through the app. The quickest way for them is Venmo.

**Add your Venmo username.** On **My games**, under **Getting paid**, choose **Add Venmo username**. Enter it as it appears in Venmo under your name (Me tab), without the @; you can also paste your Venmo profile link. Check it carefully: programs send money to this account. Directors who owe you see it, with a **Pay with Venmo** button. You're emailed whenever it changes, so if you ever get that email and didn't make the change, tell the Referee Assignor straight away.

![Getting paid](/help/img/ref-getting-paid.png)

**Your Venmo code.** Choose **My Venmo code** at the top of My games (or **Show my code**). Hold it up and the person paying scans it with their phone's camera; it opens your Venmo profile so they can pay you.

![Your Venmo code](/help/img/ref-venmo-code.png)

**When a program pays you,** they mark it as paid and you get an email. Some programs prepay the Referee Assignor instead; then the assignor pays you, and the card says which programs' reserves it came from (one payment can cover games for several programs). A card at the top of My games shows who paid, how much, and how. Once the money has arrived, choose **Got it** so they know you received it. Paid games show **Paid ✓** under **Past games**, and confirmed payments are listed under **Payments received**.

![A payment to confirm](/help/img/ref-got-it.png)

If a payment you were told about never arrives, contact that program's director or the Referee Assignor.
