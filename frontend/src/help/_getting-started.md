## Getting started

### Signing in
1. Open the league's web address in any browser, on a computer or a phone.
2. Enter the **username** and **password** your league sent you, then **Sign in**.
3. The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password. It needs at least 10 characters, including a letter and a number.

![The sign-in page](/help/img/common-sign-in.png)

**Forgot something?** Under the sign-in box, **Forgot username** emails you your username, and **Forgot password** emails you a link to set a new one. The link works once and expires after an hour.

### Finding your way around
- **On a computer,** the main pages run across the top. Everything else is under your name in the top-right corner.
- **On a phone,** tap your initials in the top-right corner. The menu is organised into sections, and **Help**, **Change password**, and **Sign out** are always at the bottom. Tap anywhere outside the menu to close it.

![The menu on a phone](/help/img/common-phone-menu.png)

A small dot on your initials, or a number next to **Requests**, means something is waiting for you.

### Good to know
- **All times are Pacific Time,** whatever time zone your phone or computer is set to.
- **Closing the browser tab signs you out.** You can also choose **Sign out** from the menu. On a shared computer, always sign out.
- **This guide** is always under **Help** in the menu, and you can download it as a PDF from the top of this page.
