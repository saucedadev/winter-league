# Coach guide

Coaches see their teams' schedule and ask for a game to be moved when something comes up. Changes you ask for go to your Program Director, then to the other team's program, and finally to the league, so everyone involved agrees before a game moves.

{{gettingStarted}}

## Your schedule

**Menu → League → Schedule.** **My teams** shows your teams' games, with your team in bold. **Whole league** shows every game. Filter by division or team, and tick **Show past games** to look back. Your dashboard also lists your next few games.

![A coach's schedule on a phone](/help/img/coach-schedule.png)

**Change requested** on a game means someone has asked to change it and it's waiting for approval. Each game also lists the referees assigned to it, or **Referees: not assigned yet**.

## Entering the final score

After a game, a coach of either team can enter the final score. Either side can do it, so there's no need to wait for the other team.

1. On **Schedule**, find the game. From tip-off on game day, it has an **Enter score** button. Tick **Needs a score** to see only played games still waiting for one. Your dashboard also says when games need scores.
2. Enter each team's points, and optionally a note such as *Forfeit* or *Ended early*.
3. Choose **Save score**.

![Entering the final score](/help/img/coach-enter-score.png)

The score replaces "vs" on everyone's schedule, with the winning score in bold and a **Final** badge.

![A game with its final score](/help/img/coach-scored.png)

The other team's coach and director get an email with the score. If a score is wrong, choose **Edit score** to correct it, or **Clear score** to remove it. Corrections are emailed too, and every change shows in the programs' Activity. A game with a final score can't be moved or rescheduled.

## Asking to move a game

1. On **Schedule**, find the game and choose **Request change**.
2. Choose what you need:
   - **Move this game:** pick a new time from the list. Every time shown is open at one of the two teams' gyms and fits both teams' schedules. If it's at the other team's gym, you'll see that the home team changes.
   - **Swap with another game:** trade the date, time, and court with another of your team's games.
3. Give a short **reason**, e.g. *"Our gym has a school concert that night."*
4. Choose **Send request**.

![Asking for a change](/help/img/coach-request-change.png)

## Following your request

**Menu → League → Requests** shows your requests and where each one is:

1. **Your Program Director** endorses it.
2. **The other program** agrees.
3. **The league** signs off, and the game moves.

![Where a request is](/help/img/coach-requests.png)

If anyone denies it, their note explains why so you can try another time. To take a request back while it's still open, choose **Withdraw request**. You'll get an email when a request is approved or denied.
